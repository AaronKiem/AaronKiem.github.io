title: Hardware V2.0
---

With this system many issues were discovered in its making in that the decoupling capacitors were in series when they needed to be in parellel and that the SPI that connected the sensor to the PIC used unroutable pins. But with the design it is possible to reroute the pins with out of board connections.

If a version 2.0 were to be made for this system, I would have more attention to connecting the pins and small components correctly. Additionally I would have placed more test pins and test header pins to have made the troubleshooting much easier. The routing would also look much cleaner from having discovered inter-layer routing.
